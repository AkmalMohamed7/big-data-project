Python tāi-seng sī 1989 nî Guido van Rossum ūi tio̍h siat-kè thang thè-ōaⁿ ABC gí-giân ê gí-giân, beh ēng tī Amoeba chit khoán chok-gia̍p hē-thóng ē-té. Van Rossum sī Python ê chú-iāu chok-chiá, sī Python gí-giân khai-hoat ê chú-chhî-chiá.
